# Rory’s Grade 3 subject catalog correction

The Subjects page previously showed the same 12-card California Grade 7 catalog as Brody’s portal. Rory’s header, diagnostic battery, assignments, and seven-week ELA course were already Grade 3; the error was limited to the separate subject catalog and one parent Science note.

Rory’s catalog now presents the seven elementary course areas used for California grades 1–6:

- English Language Arts
- Mathematics
- Science
- History–Social Science
- Physical Education
- Visual & Performing Arts
- Health Education

The first four cards identify California Grade 3 explicitly. The other three use Grade 3 or elementary labels and age-appropriate topics. The secondary catalog entries for World Language, Applied Arts, Career Technical Education, middle-school Computer Science, and Driver Education have been removed from Rory’s page. Brody’s Grade 7 catalog remains in Brody’s portal.

Topics were checked against the California Department of Education’s current Grade 3 [English Language Arts](https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/ela?dl=0&maxgrade=3&mingrade=3&order=0&page=0&perpage=10), [Mathematics](https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/math?c0=0%2C24%2C20%2C1%2C21%2C15&page=3), [Science](https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/science?c0=28%2C28%2C26%2C27), [History–Social Science](https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/history?c0=12%2C4&dl=0&maxgrade=12&mingrade=0&order=0&perpage=100), [Arts](https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/arts?c0=5&dl=0&maxgrade=12&mingrade=3&order=0&perpage=10), and [Physical Education](https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/pe?c0=11%2C11%2C3%2C7%2C11%2C0&page=3) standards. The seven-area structure follows California Education Code section 51210 for grades 1–6.

This update does not migrate, delete, or rename browser storage. Existing assignments, assessments, diagnostic attempts, logs, portfolio items, parent notes, and ELA evidence remain unchanged. Subject menus continue to include names used by existing saved records, even when those names are no longer catalog cards.

## Verification

A focused browser test checks the seven Grade 3 cards and their topics, confirms that no Grade 7, middle-school, CTE, or driver-education labels appear in Rory’s Subjects view, checks the corrected parent Science note, and verifies that synthetic saved records survive reload. It also checks the Grade 3 mobile layout and confirms that Brody’s separate local portal still displays its Grade 7 catalog.
